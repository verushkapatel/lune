What Lune keeps, and why.

Without an account

You can open scores, play them, see the notes named and practise without signing in. Everything you do then (your Repertoire, bar notes, ratings, plans and chats) is saved in your own browser, on your own device. Lune’s servers never see it.

Scores you open from your own files are read inside your browser. Lune sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.

With an account

An account lets your practice follow you between devices. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code; there is no password. Your data is stored in Lune’s database, locked so that only your account can read it.

Who you areYour email address, and a name if you give one.
Your practiceThe pieces in your Repertoire, how far through each you are, your bar notes and ratings, when each bar is due back, your plans and your piano schedule.
Your settingsYour goals and practice days, whether you use Lune as a pianist or a teacher, and your reading and access choices.
Lune AI chatsYour most recent conversations with Lune AI, so they are there on every device.
Scores you addA score you upload goes into your private storage only if you add it to your Repertoire while signed in.
TeachersYour Studio: the names and levels of the students you add, the homework you send, and, if you add one, a student’s email address for homework (see Homework).

Lune AI

Lune AI is for people with an account. When you ask it something, Lune sends your question and what it needs to answer well to Lune’s server on Cloudflare. That means the score facts for the bar or piece, and your notes, ratings and plans for that piece. The server passes them to a model run by Cloudflare Workers AI and returns the answer. Lune’s server does not store or log your questions.

When you speak to Lune AI, the recording is sent to the same server to be turned into text, and is not kept. Replies read aloud are made the same way.

Lune’s built-in answers, which work without Lune AI, are worked out on your device. If you connect a model on your own computer in Settings, your questions go to that address and nowhere else.

Emails from Lune

Lune only emails you if you turn an email on. To send one, Lune keeps the time and days you chose and your time zone.

  • The morning email brings the day’s practice from your schedule, the bars due back and a short note written by Lune AI from those facts.
  • The pre-lesson summary (for teachers) says which students practised and which bars they found hard, from the homework check-ins.

Emails are sent through Resend. Each carries an Unsubscribe link, and Gmail and other mail apps show their own one-tap Unsubscribe beside the sender. Neither needs you to sign in.

Homework

Homework links

When a teacher sends homework, the piece, the bars and the teacher’s note travel inside the link itself. A student needs no account to open it.

Check-ins

When a student taps Mark practised, or finishes practising homework, Lune records a check-in so the teacher can see how the week went. A check-in holds only a first name (if the student gives one), how each assigned bar went, and the time. It is tied to that piece of homework, not to any account, and every check-in is deleted after 120 days.

Homework by email

A teacher may add a student’s email address, or a parent’s, so that Lune sends the student their newest homework on a schedule the teacher sets: every day, or on chosen days, at a chosen time. For this Lune keeps the student’s name as the teacher typed it, the email address, and the schedule. Lune uses the address only to send that homework, and the email says which teacher set it up.

  • Every homework email has a one-tap Unsubscribe link. After that, Lune sends that address nothing more.
  • The address is deleted when the teacher removes the student from their Studio, turns the email off, or deletes their account.

Teachers: please add only an address you have permission to use. For a child under 13, use a parent’s or guardian’s address.

Microphone and voice

The microphone is used only while you ask Lune to listen, and Lune never records in the background. Listening to your playing happens in your browser. Speaking a note may use your browser’s own speech recognition, which its maker may provide (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Talking to Lune AI works as described under Lune AI.

Sharing and feedback

When you share a plan or your progress, Lune makes a page with only what you chose to share. Anyone with the link can see it.

Feedback you send from the Feedback form is emailed to Lune’s maker through FormSubmit, and may be kept in Lune’s database. It holds only what you type and the options you pick; the form does not ask for your email address.

Who helps run Lune

Lune is built on a few services. Each handles only what its job needs.

SupabaseAccounts, sign-in codes, the database and private score storage.
CloudflareLune AI’s server and model (Workers AI), and delivering the website.
GitHub PagesHosting the website’s files.
ResendSending the emails you or your teacher turned on.
FormSubmitPassing on feedback you choose to send.

Scores, piano sounds and fonts load from lune.page itself.

Children

Many pianists who use Lune are young. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before making an account. Students never need an account to use homework from a teacher.

Lune AI questionsNot kept by Lune’s server. Chats are saved to your account so you can come back to them, until you delete them.
Without an accountIn your browser until you clear it.

Your choices

  • Download everything from Settings, then your account.
  • Delete your account from the same place. It removes your practice, chats, uploaded scores, Studio and email settings together.
  • Delete a chat from the list beside Lune AI.
  • Stop any email with its Unsubscribe link, or in Settings.
  • Use Lune without an account, and your practice stays on your device.

Questions about your data?
