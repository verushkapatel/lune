The maker

I’m Verushka, fifteen, an ABRSM Grade 7 pianist. Sight reading and fingering used to stall my practice, so I built Lune: open a score, hear it, see letter names or finger numbers, and work the bars that will not sit yet.

Lune has a free catalogue, braille music, an AI teacher that reads the bar in front of you, and it works offline. The next step is getting it into the hands of many more pianists and teachers, and that needs partners.
